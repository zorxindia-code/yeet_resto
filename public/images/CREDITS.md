# Photo credits

## Menu photos (`/images/menu/`)

Cut out of the client's own menu poster, supplied on 2026-11-25 as the source for the menu. Each file is the poster's
photo of that item, lifted onto transparency with Apple's Vision framework and resampled 2×. No food details were
generated or painted in. Several cups and cartons carry another brand's crown logo and are flagged for replacement in
`lib/menu.ts` (`imageNote`).

## Stand-in photography (everything else)

Temporary demo photography for the UI phase. Every photo is CC0 (public domain dedication),
sourced through [Openverse](https://openverse.org) from StockSnap and rawpixel, downloaded and
re-encoded locally. Cutouts were made locally with Apple's Vision subject-lifting.
Replace with YEET's own photography before launch.
